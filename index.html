🕹️ How To Use
  • Drag → Rotate 3D view
  • Scroll → Zoom in/out
  • 📷 Enter AR → iPhone camera
  • Move phone to detect surface
  • Tap "Place Object" → drops 3D object
  • 🥽 Enter VR → Oculus headset
⚙️ How It Works

Three.js builds a 3D scene using WebGL on your GPU. WebXR API connects it to your iPhone camera (AR) or Oculus headset (VR). Hit-test detects real flat surfaces so 3D objects sit on your table or floor.
